About

I'm a PhD student at Arizona State Univeristy, where I work with Prof. Hokeun Kim at KIM Lab on cyber-physical systems and embodied AI. My research broadly works towards creating mechanisms and tools to ensure efficient, safe and deterministic systems.

Before I started my PhD, I completed my MS at ASU, where I focused on reconfigurable systems. I care about building systems that engage with the physical world without introducing chaos.
